# Cloudi

Cloudi is a cute virtual desktop companion that lives on your screen and reacts to what you do.

## Features

* Sleeps and wakes up
* Thought bubbles and random reactions
* Different moods and reactions
* Weather-based interactions
* Mini-games and activities
* Achievements and secrets
* Optional AI conversations

## Design

Cloudi is designed to be simple, cute, and expressive, with a fluffy cloud appearance and animated eyes.

## Goal

Cloudi is made to make your desktop feel more alive by giving you a small companion that reacts, plays, and occasionally surprises you.

**Your desktop. Your Cloudi.**
